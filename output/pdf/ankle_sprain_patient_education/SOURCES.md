## Selection notes

- Files 01-04 and 08-12 are patient-facing educational materials from official NHS trust domains.
- Files 05-07 are peer-reviewed evidence syntheses hosted by their publishers and indexed in the U.S. National Library of Medicine's PubMed Central.
- Together they cover ankle-sprain basics, early management, progressive activity, rehabilitation exercises, physical therapy evidence, reinjury prevention, warning signs, and when to seek professional help.
- The PDFs contain general education, not individualized diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Keep the original source URL and document date as metadata when ingesting these files into the RAG system.

## Added sources 13-32

- Files 13-15, 19, and 25-30 are original PDFs published on the issuing
  institution's official website.
- Files 16-18, 20-24, 31, and 32 are local PDF snapshots of official
  patient-education webpages. Each snapshot has a provenance cover page with
  its title, institution, publication/update date, and original URL.
- Snapshot text was copied in English from the official page. Processing only
  removed website navigation and normalized whitespace; it did not translate,
  paraphrase, or add medical instructions.
- The stated dates are preserved even when a document is older or its review
  date has passed, so users can judge currency rather than having it hidden.

## Added sources 33-42

- Files 34 and 35 are original PDFs from the issuing institutions. Files
  33 and 36-42 are English-text PDF snapshots of official pages, each with a
  provenance cover page.
- These sources were selected to strengthen retrieval about injury grading,
  staged rehabilitation, functional testing, return to running, and return to
  court or field sports.
- The snapshot process removed website navigation and normalized whitespace
  only. It did not translate or rewrite the source medical content.
